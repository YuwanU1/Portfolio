ABOUT

I am a second-year BSIT student at PLV, currently building my skills in programming, data analysis, and problem-solving. Even though I’m naturally a logical thinker, I always try to balance that with creativity. One of my biggest passions is photography—I love capturing centered, clean, and symmetrical compositions, whether it’s for personal projects or event coverage. I’ve worked on several photography sets, posters, and event collections, and I’m continuously exploring ways to improve my style and create more visually balanced images. Outside academics, I enjoy diving into different forms of art. I like experimenting with pixel art, exploring music, and expressing creativity through design. In my free time, I also enjoy playing video games, reading manga, and watching anime, which all inspire my sense of aesthetics and storytelling. As a tech student with an artistic side, I’m always eager to learn new things—whether it’s mastering a coding concept, improving my photography techniques, or experimenting with creative projects for my portfolio. My goal is to keep growing both logically and creatively while building a future in IT and digital media.
